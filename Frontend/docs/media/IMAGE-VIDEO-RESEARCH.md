# Bonne Assise — image & video research register

This register distinguishes source research from cleared production assets. Do not publish a remotely sourced image merely because it is visible on a website; confirm the specific file's licence, attribution, model/privacy considerations and any commercial restrictions first.

## Priority sources

1. **Official Benin resources** — https://benin.bj/ressources
   - Offers official image/video resources, including landscape footage and materials from cultural festivals.
   - Treat usage conditions as specific to each asset; the page explicitly notes restrictions on commercial use of country-brand materials without prior authorization. Ask for written permission for any hero/brand usage that could imply endorsement.

## Selection rules

- Hero: prefer one authentic, high-resolution Benin image with a clear focal point and negative space for readable text. Test desktop and mobile crops separately.
- Food: use identifiable Beninese dishes, not generic stock food. Include the dish name, location/region when known, author, source URL, licence and any edits in `ATTRIBUTION.csv`.
- People: confirm consent and context before editorial or commercial use, especially for identifiable children, ceremonies and sacred practices.
- Video: prefer a licensed original film or a written permission agreement. Do not extract frames from third-party videos without rights clearance.
- Sponsored placements: label them clearly and keep sponsorship separate from editorial verification.
- Do not present illustrative/AI-generated imagery as documentary evidence of a real person, place, dish or event. If used as illustration, label it accordingly.
