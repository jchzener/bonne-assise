# Bonne Assise hero film — storyboard and rights workflow

## Creative direction
- Working title: **Live Benin. / Vivez le Bénin.**
- Runtime target: 12–16 seconds, seamless loop, no baked-in text, no audio.
- Format: 16:9 master (minimum 1920×1080), with safe framing for 9:16 / narrow mobile crops.
- Visual rule: no Amazon imagery, no generic rainforest montage, no staged cultural cliché.
- Narrative: a real food-making gesture → urban everyday life / market → architecture or craft → water/coast or northern landscape → people sharing a meal.
- The footage should feel observed rather than staged. Prioritize Beninese creators and locally sourced footage.

## Shot list
1. 0–2s: close-up of a real cooking gesture, steam or ingredients; not a generic stock-food close-up.
2. 2–4s: market exchange or everyday street scene in Cotonou.
3. 4–6s: craft, architecture or a lived-in street in Porto-Novo / Ouidah.
4. 6–8s: lake/lagoon life at Ganvié or So-Ava, framed as one place among many.
5. 8–11s: coast, fishing activity or a landscape from another region; avoid rainforest clichés.
6. 11–15s: shared meal / human connection, cut to a composition that loops naturally.

Use this video for internal visual research and shot-list inspiration only until written permission is obtained from the rights holder. Do not extract and publish video segments, still frames, soundtrack, or thumbnails on Bonne Assise merely because the video is publicly viewable. Ask the producer/rightsholder for explicit commercial web, social, editing, territory, duration and derivative-work permissions. Music rights must be cleared separately.

## Rights clearance checklist (required before publication)
- [ ] Identify producer, videographer, and rights holder.
- [ ] Written license covers commercial use on website and social channels.
- [ ] Written permission covers edits, crops, frame extraction and looping.
- [ ] Music and any recognizable performers / people have required releases.
- [ ] Record source URL, creator, license, expiry/territory, proof of permission and attribution.
- [ ] Store cleared master under `public/videos/benin-hero.mp4`; do not commit unlicensed source files.

## Technical delivery
- MP4/H.264, muted, no audio track if possible; target under 5 MB, ideally 2–4 MB.
- Provide a compressed WebM alternative only if testing shows a benefit.
- Poster image must be licensed independently and optimized as WebP/AVIF where supported.
- Video must not delay initial page render; `preload="none"`, `playsInline`, muted loop.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; the poster remains the fallback.
- Keep the only overlay copy to the localized title, no badge, subtitle, CTA or photo caption.
